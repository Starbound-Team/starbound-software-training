# Intro

*Ship's log, Daedalus-2199. Automated entry, read on wake.*

Welcome back, captain.

The Daedalus-2199 has reached its destination: an uncharted planet on the outer edges of the solar system, Neo-Adromeda, and the reason for this whole trip. A few days ago the ship launched its research drone down
to the surface to scan, sample and report back everything it finds.

It is your mission that the research drone can report successfully all of the
unusual conditions that seem to surround this planet. *The mission was rushed, so not everything may go as expected.*

The Daedalus-2199 flies a long **elliptical orbit** around the planet. For the
next **two weeks** the ship stays close enough to talk to the drone. After
that it swings out of range, so whatever you may need to do to the drone, you
should consider doing fast.

The onboard system, **BoundStar OS**, will hand you the repair jobs as
missions. Each one comes in two parts:

- **Part a** sets up the problem. Solve it and log your answer.
- **Part b** appears under part a once you've solved it. It usually goes a
  step further, so keep part a in mind.

Type your answers into the box under each mission. If you're close, the log
may give you a nudge. Your progress is saved between shifts.

*Press esc to return to the mission list.*
